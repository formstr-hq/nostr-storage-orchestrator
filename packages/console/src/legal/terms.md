# Terms & Conditions

## 1. The service

Blossom provides content-addressed blob storage over the Blossom HTTP protocol. Plans grant a total storage allowance and a duration. There is no free tier; a paid or granted entitlement is required before any upload.

## 2. Plans, renewal, and upgrades

- Buying the same plan again adds its duration to your expiry.
- Upgrading applies the larger allowance immediately and adds the purchased duration on top of your remaining days.
- A lower-tier plan can only be purchased once your current plan has expired; you then enter a grace period if your data exceeds the new allowance.

## 3. Lifecycle and purge

After expiry: grace period, then uploads blocked, then reads blocked, then purge. Timings are published in the lifecycle configuration. Renew at any point before purge to keep your data.

## 4. Acceptable use

You must not store illegal content, malware, or material that infringes third-party rights. We may remove blobs that violate these terms; removals are logged and the owner is notified with the reason.

## 5. Availability

The service is provided as-is. We aim for durable storage but you are responsible for keeping your own copies of anything you cannot afford to lose.

## 6. Changes

Terms are versioned. A purchase records the version you accepted. Continued use after a version bump constitutes acceptance of the new version.
